# Client refinement through 2015

October 24, 2026. Local work requested by the user, continuing from the TI2 pass to the start of 2016. Nothing committed, pushed or deployed. This reviews the game's annual composite patches and dated menu changes inside them; it does not claim to recreate every weekly Valve update. Claude's later-era work shares this checkout and was preserved.

These are functional historical adaptations, not pixel-perfect archived clients. Composite hero pools and combat data remain unchanged.

## Follow-up review (October 24, Claude)

The user asked Claude to review this work and fix the gaps it found against the captures.

- **January 2013 Today:** the capture shows Valve's Troll Warlord key art as the blog banner and the post "No, no problems here." with its inline image. Both now show, taken from that Steam post (January 24, 7:33 pm PST). The same banner and post treatment covers Valve's next 2013 hero posts: Bristleback (March 28), Skywrath Mage (April 19), Elder Titan (May 2) and Beastmaster (June 21). Each stays up until the next post, and the last ends on September 18, the day before First Blood (a text-only post). Only the January banner is confirmed by a capture; the others' run lengths are inferred. Events and TI screens take priority.
- **TI3 Compendium cover:** the left page is now blank, as in the capture. The right page has the capture's pale band between dark rules, with red "The top sixteen teams in the world compete for the Dota 2 championship" and "Main event August 7–11, 2013 · Benaroya Hall, home of the Seattle Symphony · Seattle, Washington, USA". The paper has a generated mottled texture; it is not Valve's paper.
- **Diretide 2013:** the card title is Valve's gothic Diretide logo, cut from the 2012 event page background (`diretide/bg_01.jpg`) with the black removed, in place of plain green text.
- Left as is: the Wraith-Night progress label reads "The road to The International 2014" (a deliberate campaign adaptation of "The Awakening of the Wraith King").
