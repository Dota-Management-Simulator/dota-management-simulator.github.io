## Original ranges and preparation month

The **Heroes** navigation tab lists every hero available in the campaign's current release pool and patch, using phase-specific roles, bundled hero icons, name search, position filters and optional estimated-strength sorting. Strength means an uncertain equal-team win-rate outlook, not an attribute rating or a guaranteed campaign result. All bars share a labelled 0–100% scale, display the actual low/high interval and retain numeric text and accessible descriptions. Overlapping intervals do not establish an exact ranking.

General catalog observations have a stable 16-point spread. Already-discovered favorites reuse the existing four-point patch report exactly. Wider reads enclose every possible four-point read for that target, so staff discovery narrows the original interval; release and rehire cannot reroll it. Hidden favorites are not identified in broad observations. Catalog estimates are UI-only: auto-drafting retains its existing staff-limited report, and combat coefficients are unchanged. These estimates describe game balance targets; they do not certify measured engine balance or reproduce real professional win rates.

The main **Patch & meta** summary now shows current and previous interval bars for all seven gameplay axes, with semantic low/high endpoints and distinct labels/colors. Discovered hero changes and the detailed favorites list also have range bars. Existing axis uncertainty and specialist widths are unchanged.

New dated campaigns start one **calendar month before the catalogued TI opening**, at UTC midnight, clamping the day to the preceding month's length. TI1 starts **July 17, 2011**, before its August 17 opening. Years without a catalogued TI, including cancelled TI2020 and uncatalogued future years, retain January 1; no tournament date is invented. Setup previews the opening date and explains that fallback. Subsequent calendar progression stays continuous through TI, the shuffle and the next year. Existing dated saves are never rewound. Legacy weekly saves keep their clock unless the manager explicitly enables the dated calendar, which uses the new opening rule.

The preparation month uses normal weekly training, world development, wages, recovery, sponsor and contract timing. Creating a campaign charges nothing and grants no retrospective development. Four full weeks fit before TI1, and Continue stops at the invitation's opening after the remaining three days, without charging a fifth week. Entering a distant tournament directly still advances and settles all genuinely elapsed weeks. Later starts may begin after that year's qualifiers have closed; qualification rules and historical event dates remain authoritative.
