# Terrain, trees and exact ward placement

Added for the October 26–27, 2026 combat update. The user's Scrim Circuit Tracker supplied the reference approach: shared terrain facts, elevation/tree shadowcasting, and a distinction between normal vision and true-sight detection.

Eight eras (`700`, `707`, `720`, `723`, `732`, `733`, `738`, `740`) import pinned Valve terrain exports from [leamare/dota-map-coordinates](https://github.com/leamare/dota-map-coordinates). The exporter combines ground height, tree tops, walkability, vision blockers and prohibited ward areas in tiled PNGs. `scripts/import-map-terrain.mjs` downloads and SHA-256 records those files, then creates a compact factual grid. `scripts/import-map-structures.mjs` separately imports the matching building positions. Raw files stay under ignored `.local/terrain-source/`. Source URLs and hashes are in [terrain sources](map-terrain-sources.json) and [building sources](map-structure-sources.json). These ledgers establish provenance, not a blanket license for Valve artwork or an endorsement by the exporter.

World coordinates transform to battle coordinates as `x = 400 + worldX / 20`, `y = 400 - worldY / 20`. Source Roshan landmarks agree with existing artwork pit anchors within roughly six battle units on the checked 7.40 overview. Tree overlays were inspected against six representative map illustrations. Source building anchors correct the larger offsets in the earlier CSS-icon-origin placements. All 288 sourced structures across the eight eras sit outside the imported tree/blocker tiles.

The three earlier maps (`pre682`, `682`, `686`) have no recovered full terrain export. `early-map-terrain.ts` traces tree-grove polygons from each bundled overview, including separate era revisions. Ground, river and base heights are bounded approximations. These are grove footprints, **not individual recovered historical trees**. Ward previews explicitly identify traced/approximate terrain. Neither later trees nor random decorative trees are presented as exact earlier history.

`map-vision.ts` supplies the shared shadowcasting footprint to bots, human visibility filtering, the fog mask and ward previews. Trees and higher ground block normal sight; lower terrain and lower canopies can be seen from higher ground. An observer has a scaled 1,600-world-unit sight radius. A sentry's detection radius does not reveal ordinary ground on its own. Its circular preview is labelled detection rather than vision.

Legal ward cliffs can be unwalkable: the placement check uses the source no-ward layer, trees and blockers, while the player casts from nearby reachable ground. A click is never silently moved. Invalid clicks show a reason. Ground-unit movement uses connected walkable cells and bounded cached A* routes without diagonal corner cutting. The route cache stores one path per (start cell, goal cell), so a cached route always equals a fresh search. Battles running in the same worker can't change each other's routes. An earlier cache merged paths by destination, and two identical seeded battles could diverge depending on what else had run (fixed October 27, 2026; regression test in `tests/match-events.test.ts`). Spawn, respawn and teleport destinations may project to nearby reachable ground; ward destinations remain exactly where ordered.

All standing trees remain static. Tree cutting/regrowth, complete destruction interactions, flying vision, invisibility on all heroes, exact collision hulls and each historical day/night exception are outside this implementation. The earliest three eras and outer terrain absent from the overview remain approximations. Existing combat attack/spell ranges and movement retain their scaled manager-engine rules; this is not a claim of full spatial parity with Valve's game. Terrain/structure content signatures invalidate prior balance coefficients and experiment fingerprints.
