# Tournament Battle Pass companions

October 24, 2026 follow-up. Every Battle Pass promotion already advertised by the Reborn client opens its cosmetic reward collection and a usable tournament companion. The latest user request makes the original sets, Arcanas, Personas and scrollable reward galleries the default experience. This supersedes the purchase-screen-only interaction, the generic contents modal and the tournament-only default. The Source 1 TI3–TI5 books retain their separate historical designs.

## Original reward showcases

The opening **Highlights** page selects a headline Arcana or prestige reward. Each has a large original reveal illustration, original branding where available, hero and reward type, a short description, the original unlock milestone and next/previous controls. **Rewards** opens the complete imported collection with search by hero/name, collection and type filters, a horizontally scrollable reward track and a vertically scrollable gallery. **Treasures** isolates treasure contents. Every item opens the same large inspection stage. The original tournament pages remain under **Tournament**.

There are **237 cosmetic previews across eleven passes**, with **422 local artwork files** (about 31 MB) and **192 verified animation clips**. The galleries cover:

These are cosmetic previews of the original releases. The original Dota unlock level is displayed as historical information; campaign money, readiness and tournament prizes are not substituted for Battle Points. Treasure contents, including rare/golden variants, are not presented as guaranteed level unlocks. Selecting a reward does not fabricate ownership or award an item. This feature adds no paid levels, purchases, random openings, cosmetic inventory or progression save fields. Original cosmetic content is a retrospective collection, not a reconstruction of every staggered release date inside each pass window. Collector’s Cache purchases, every consumable, voice line, physical reward and all early seasonal treasure contents are not an exhaustive archive.

The large stage offers alternate views when available. **Play preview** requests only the selected original animation, with native playback controls; Still artwork and changing reward unmount the clip. Videos pause when the stage leaves view or the tab is hidden. Artwork loads locally and gallery thumbnails load lazily. Reduced-motion settings disable smooth track scrolling; clips start only after explicit play. Failed streaming falls back to the verified still rather than replacing it with a modern base-hero render.

Sources and validation are recorded in [the reward asset ledger](battle-pass-reward-assets.json). TI2016–19 galleries were imported from the original Valve Battle Pass HTML, including their treasure-specific item names and preview media; the newer rosters/levels/names come from Valve’s website components and English localisation. Primary pages: [2016](https://www.dota2.com/international2016/battlepass), [2017](https://www.dota2.com/international2017/battlepass), [2018](https://www.dota2.com/international2018/battlepass), [2019](https://www.dota2.com/international2019/battlepass), [2020](https://www.dota2.com/international2020/battlepass), [Nemestice](https://www.dota2.com/nemestice), [Continuum](https://www.dota2.com/labyrinth), [2022](https://www.dota2.com/battlepass2022) and [Winter 2016](https://www.dota2.com/winter2016). Early seasonal thumbnails are from Steam’s item catalogue. Valve-authored item localisation, preserved in the [SteamTracking mirror](https://github.com/SteamTracking/GameTracking-Dota2/blob/master/game/dota/pak01_dir/resource/localization/items_english.txt), identifies the Fall 2015 infusable equipment. When a full set render was unavailable, the individual Steam equipment thumbnail is labelled accordingly; it is not a replacement base-hero model. Some retired poster URLs were replaced with a still at one second of the surviving original reveal film. Invalid sources and non-cosmetic level-bundle entries are excluded.

## Coverage and behavior

| Pass | Tournament companion |
| --- | --- |
| Fall 2015 Compendium | Frankfurt Major |
| Winter 2016 Battle Pass | Shanghai Major |
| TI2016 Battle Pass | TI2016 |
| Fall 2016 Battle Pass | Boston Major |
| TI2017, TI2018, TI2019 Battle Passes | Their corresponding TIs |
| TI10 Battle Pass, released in 2020 | TI10, held in 2021 after postponement |
| Nemestice | Catalogued tournaments and qualifiers overlapping its season |
| Aghanim’s Labyrinth / Continuum Conundrum | Catalogued tournaments overlapping its season |
| 2022 Battle Pass | TI2022 |

The header crest, home promotion and pass card all open the matching guide. Archived events retain their own associated pass. A still-advertised older promotion remains openable. New dated preview entries expose the pass launch moments without changing the normal campaign clock.

Home, The tournament, The teams, Venue & schedule, The prize pool and Results & placements are real controls. Home has a feature illustration, tournament summary and five chapter tiles. Chapters retain previous/next navigation; season passes have a tournament selector. Calendar actions select the exact tournament. Teams and preparation links open the existing management pages. Escape, close and Back to dashboard dismiss the native modal, which contains focus and restores it to the opener.

Pinned campaign rules, lineups, fixtures, completed series and placements take precedence over the historical catalogue. A tournament without a campaign run shows a labelled reference field and an honest empty results state. The guide never invents match results. Prize figures use the existing tournament definition, including its verification notes and rounding limitations. These are tournament prizes, not new Battle Pass rewards. Opening or browsing advances no time, spends no money and writes no pass progression or save fields.

Season guides are a manager-game adaptation: Nemestice and Continuum were not separate historical TI tournaments. Their guide windows select overlapping catalogue records, including qualifiers; they do not establish a new tournament or certify every historical pass expiry. Named Major/TI guides remain accessible from their promotions beyond their tournament end. The Continuum guide’s February 23 endpoint follows the next advertised update and is a selection approximation.

## Visual treatment and evidence

The companion now fills the client viewport. Early passes use a compact masthead and horizontal tabs; TI2022 uses a left section list beside the hero stage, with angled chapter surfaces. Nemestice uses a violet breadcrumb strip and crystal artwork. Eleven scoped palettes follow their period artwork: green Fall 2015/Fall 2016, icy Winter 2016, warm TI2016, underwater TI2017, green TI2018, purple/gold TI2019, gold TI10, violet Nemestice, blue Continuum and ember TI2022. The 2020 sidebar and Continuum layout are adaptations of the surrounding Reborn patterns, not independently verified pixel copies.

Valve’s [Winter 2016 page](https://www.dota2.com/winter2016), [TI2017 page](https://www.dota2.com/international2017/battlepass), [TI2019 page](https://www.dota2.com/international2019/battlepass) and [2022 page](https://www.dota2.com/battlepass2022), including their stylesheets, supply the original feature artwork and branding. Four additional assets total about 535 KB: Winter Lich, TI2017 underwater backdrop, TI2019 forest backdrop and the TI2022 Faceless Void illustration. Sources, transformations, byte sizes and hashes are in [the asset manifest](dota-menu-assets.json). Captured users, chat, cosmetic inventories and purchase history are not copied.

The tournament chapter controls remain a manager-game adaptation. The reward showcases above now provide cosmetic reveals and original animation previews; paid levels, wagering, fantasy scoring, cavern crawls and predictions remain unimplemented. Browsing a reveal does not grant an Arcana. Original website art is adapted to the client stage; exact historical page geometry and every subsection are not certified. The 2022 companion currently uses its first-part ember treatment throughout the guide’s availability.
