## Parked event-mode ideas

Saved for later (2026-10-24). Don't build these until the user asks.
Built already: the OpenAI bots challenge and the TI All-Star match (see `docs/event-modes.md`).

1. Diretide / Greeviling candy war: during the event window, an objectives-over-kills bonus BO1 against a rival. Uses the `menu-events.ts` windows.
2. Siltbreaker / Aghanim's Labyrinth co-op raid: your five against scripted boss waves, once a week, teaches tactics with no results at risk.
3. Wraith-Night defence: hold the Ancient for N minutes against waves.
4. Ability Draft or All Random weekends: random drafts test player hero pools; practice value like scrims, small fan boost.
5. Captain's Draft cup: restricted-pool weekend bracket against AI clubs.
6. 1v1 mid tournaments (TI7/TI8 solo events): needs a 1v1 engine, which would also make the TI7 OpenAI 1v1 bot playable.
7. Compendium predictions: predict TI results for fan or cash rewards, on the existing TI screens.
8. Legends showmatch: retired players return for one exhibition; you can sign one afterwards.
9. Crownfall chapter quests: chapter goals (win X scrims, research Y heroes) give one-off rewards.

Ways to take part, cheapest first: an inbox invitation on the real date, sign-up through the registration/reservation flow, qualifying through rank or results, or an open Arcade entry with a daily or weekly limit.
