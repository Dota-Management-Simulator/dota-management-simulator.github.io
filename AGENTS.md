## Era scale fix and playstyle meta pull — October 30, 2026

**Local, not published:** two changes.

- **Era scale.** It now counts only years whose tournament list includes tier 2. 2024–2026 listed tier 1 only, which put them at the 6× cap. They now hold 4.1, so a 2025 start has $410k cash and $24.5k/week payroll. No post-2023 contraction is modelled.
- **Playstyle meta pull.** At each post-TI shuffle, every player's playstyles drift toward what that year's patches rewarded, compared with each patch's own axis average. Adaptability sets the speed.
- **Meta fit.** Fit with the current patch gives both sides up to ±2 decision-skill points in legacy events and dated tournaments (`BattlePlayer.metaFit`, player control only).

- **Tighter economy.** Sponsors now pay 40% of their catalog value weekly (was 50%), and LAN travel is $750 × scale per player (was $600). Default-start sponsor cover is 26–54% outside 2019 (76%), and runway without prizes is 13–37 weeks.

See [economy](docs/economy.md#era-scale) and [player careers](docs/player-careers.md#era-meta-pull-on-playstyles--october-30-2026). Not balance-calibrated. No commit, push or deployment.

## Pay mix and pressure — October 29, 2026

**Local, not published:** contracts trade guaranteed pay against prize shares. Each player can take up to 20% (five players can take 100%), and pay can be $0. The club keeps the rest of the prize money and still pays travel, staff and camps. ## Player market, egos and team chemistry — October 27, 2026

**Local, not published:** a one-season playtest found every historical player copied their role template's fee/wage (`makePlayer` in `history.ts`), so the best carry cost the same $10k + $1,500/wk as the worst. `src/player-market.ts` now prices players by skill rank in the whole pool (about $600–$3,600/wk), adjusted for active innates, hero pool and recent form; fees are six weeks of value, ×1.5 buyout from another club. Seeded ego (1–10) and self-awareness make players overvalue or undervalue themselves; aware stars with earned leverage ask for it. Once the club is winning (starters' form 55%+ or reputation 60+), stars and big egos demand 1–10% prize shares. Renewals reprice at current value. New campaigns start starters at market pay (2011 Na'Vi: $8,840/wk vs the old $6,000). Old saves keep their current payroll. The Squad page has a team rating/synergy panel (playstyle cohesion, lanes, hero pools, innate support, personalities, time together); the market shows fee, ask and FIT; the contract panel shows value, ask, ego, self-image, prize demand and fit. Synergy now feeds matches as a capped team combat bonus of ±0.04 (±5 OVR; same term as difficulty and form) for both sides in legacy events, dated tournaments including AI-vs-AI worker fixtures, scrims and All-Star; the bot challenge applies it to your side only. Time together counts only finished events, so bonuses stay fixed within a running tournament. Not balance-calibrated. Wiring it exposed an existing determinism bug: `nextWalkableWaypoint`'s module-level route cache merged next-hop paths by destination, so identical seeded battles diverged depending on which other battles had run in the process (live/replay recovery and worker-vs-watched equality depend on that). The cache is now a pure per-(start cell, goal cell) memo; a 2011 map ran 7.5–7.8s against 8.1–12.6s before. Map durations for a given seed change, and combat outcomes for existing seeds may differ. Sponsors, prizes and other costs are unchanged. Welfare's roster-observation table is now built lazily to break a new import cycle. See [player market](docs/player-market.md). Four existing tests now use computed fees/asks instead of template values; two tournament-strength assertions include the synergy term. No commit, push or deployment.
